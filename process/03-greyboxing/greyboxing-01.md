# Greyboxing 01 (21.05.26)

Greyboxing, as a rapid prototyping technique, allowed us to explore the concept in a simple 3D environment using Unity. We used basic geometric shapes such as cubes, rectangles, and cylinders while experimenting with initial interactions, including object snapping, zooming into objects, and object breaking.

Two different café layouts were explored: one more cubic and compact (1), and another larger layout with a more complex, layered structure (2). By comparing both layouts side by side, it quickly became apparent that the second version felt more organic and natural. This led to the realization that we wanted to incorporate this direction (2) into the final design of our indoor café scene.
