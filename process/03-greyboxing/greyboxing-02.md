# Greyboxing 02 (06.06.26)

The second and final greybox prototype was developed over multiple days using placeholder furniture and objects to create a clearer sense of the spatial layout and atmosphere. Step by step, additional interactions were implemented and refined throughout the process. Through multiple iterations, different shaders and shadow settings were also explored to better define the visual direction of the experience.
