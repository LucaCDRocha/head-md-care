# First Story Ideas 

## The last bite 
The last pastry is prepared just before closing forever. The bakery’s most loyal customer reserves not an object, but the final pastry made by the beloved Sonia.
To make the moment unforgettable, he asks for the pastry to be red - a colour chosen to become a memory. (Red colour = remembrance of many red objects in the place). And instead of simply receiving it, he surprises Sonia by asking to make it together with her.

## Americano day
It is the 4th of July, and Sonia casually asks customers to add something “American” to the café walls before ordering. Some leave postcards, others scribble song lyrics, baseball references, motel sketches, fast-food wrappers, movie quotes, or cigarette packs from old trips.
As the night goes on, the café slowly changes into a messy, strange version of America built entirely from personal obsessions and half-true memories.
What Sonia (name to be assigned) creates is less a celebration of a country, and more a portrait of how people quietly use places to project parts of themselves they cannot show anywhere else.

<br>

## Same place, same time
An older customer, Marko, who lives alone, comes to the café every morning as part of his routine: same place, same time, same order, same chair. Sabine already prepares his drink before he arrives. One day, Marko does not come. Sabine starts wondering where he is. There is no message, no call, no answer. She becomes increasingly worried when she still hasn’t heard from him the next day. She gathers everyone together to start looking for him, as if he were a family member. She closes the café for the time being. It is only because of their close bond that it becomes noticeable that something has happened to Marko.

## (no name)
It is the opening of the bakery, and people come in; it is a happy moment.
We jump forward in time, and a new customer arrives. He falls in love with the place and starts coming regularly over several days. One day, he does not come, and both the café and the owner become worried. However, the next day he returns, and the place feels bright and alive again.We then see different moments over time with the same person continuing to visit. At some point, it is revealed that the place has a closing date, and people become sad. They decide that on the last day of opening, they will hold a party to be together in this place one more time. So what will happen after?
