# Onboarding

## First seconds (before 1st interaction)

**When the visitor approach the iPad, what is the first thing they see?**<br>
The cafe from the outside (door, window (view inside), greenery, bench, Cafe name, open sign)


**Is there something standing out?**<br>
Focus on the door (maybe little animation of handle)

**What is hidden?**<br> 
the cafe from inside, objects 


**Is there any sounds?**<br>
rain + sound from inside (talking, coffee machine, cutlery in use) but muffled 

**How does you world “feel”? What is the general mood?**<br>
outside: cold, dull, lonely, raining<br>
inside: warm, cozy, comforting, inviting, wholesome

## First Interaction

**What is the first action the user have to do?**<br> 
Clicking on the door (door opens on release → finger up)

**How do they know what they should do?**<br>
intuition, highlighted door, handle slightly moving, (+optional open sign moving/blinking)

**What happens if they don’t?**<br> 
nothing, time does not progress

**Do I show the user what to do? If yes, how?**<br>
Sign saying: Come in!

**How does the user feel when they accomplished their first action?**<br>
great! (; → you feel like entering your home<br>
transition from cold, sad to warm, comfortable<br>

**What happens next? → interactions that have to be implemented**<br> 
→ Door (Click → on release)<br>
→ Drink Coffee (Click  → on release)<br>
→ Puzzle Scattered Object (Drag & Drop → if in the right position, piece will snap in)<br>
→ Zoom in on appearing objects (Click on object → on release)<br>
→ Zoom out on appearing objects (Click around object → on release)<br>
→ Finding hidden pieces (Click → on release)<br>
