# Onboarding

## First seconds (before 1st interaction)

- When the visitor approach the iPad, what is the first thing they see? 
  - The cafe from the outside (door, window (view inside), greenery, bench, Cafe name, open sign)

- Is there something standing out?  
    - → Focus on the door (maybe little animation of handle)

- What is hidden? 
    - → the cafe from inside, objects 

- Is there any sounds? 
    - rain + sound from inside (talking, coffee machine, cutlery in use) but muffled 

- How does you world “feel”? What is the general mood?
    - outside: cold, dull, lonely, raining 
    - inside: warm, cozy, comforting, inviting, wholesome

## First Interaction

- What is the first action the user have to do? 
    - Clicking on the door (door opens on release → finger up)

- How do they know what they should do?  
    - intuition, highlighted door, handle slightly moving, (+optional open sign moving/blinking)

- What happens if they don’t? 
    - nothing, time does not progress

- Do I show the user what to do? If yes, how? 
    - Sign saying: Come in!

- How does the user feel when they accomplished their first action?  
    - great! (; → you feel like entering your home
    - transition from cold, sad to warm, comfortable

- What happens next? → interactions that have to be implemented 
    - Door (Click → on release)
    - Drink Coffee (Click  → on release)
    - Puzzle Scattered Object (Drag & Drop → if in the right position, piece will snap in)
    - Zoom in on appearing objects (Click on object → on release)
    - Zoom out on appearing objects (Click around object → on release)
    - Finding hidden pieces (Click → on release)
