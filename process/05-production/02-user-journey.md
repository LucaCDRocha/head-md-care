# User Journey (05.06.26)

## Key Elements
**Timespan**<br>
Café opens: Autumn 2018 | Story ends: Autumn 2026 | 8 years

**Player Role**<br>
A stranger who wanders in from the rain for the first time; breaks an object by accident. Repairing it piece by piece, allows to move through the café's history, and to discover what is about to be lost.

**Puzzle Structure**<br>
7 pieces, each placed moves time 

**Closing Hints**<br> 
appear only in 2026 scenes

<br> 

## Scenes

SCENE 1 — Outside the Café <br>
*Autumn 2026 (present day)*
- **What the player sees:** the Café from outside with the Name “Maria’s Garden“; rain; warm light glows from inside through the window; the door has a sign “Come In”
- **Player action:** Tap / click on the door
- **What happens:** Door swings open → Transition to Scene 2
- **Audio:** Rain

<br>
<br>

SCENE 2 — At the Table <br>
*Autumn 2026 (present day) → 2018*
- **What the player sees:** Point-of-view from a seat at a table; a cup of coffee in front of the player; a menu lies on the table beside it and a amphora sits at the edge; shelves are filled with objects
- **What the player hears:** Maria (owner) introduces herself 
- **Player action:**  Tap / click the cup
- **What happens:** Cub lifts, sip, cup moves back down toward the table → It nudges the amphora, which breaks → calendar begins turning back ... 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020, 2019, 2018 → simultaneously the shelves empty, objects disappear → one piece lands on the table, at the exact moment the time element lands on autumn 2018
- **Audio:** café ambient, coffe drinking, amphora bracking, rewind time
- **Closing hint:** On the menu, multiple drinks are crossed out 

<br>
<br>

SCENE 3 — Puzzle Piece 1 | Object: Barny the Frog <br>
*2018 → Spring 2019*
- **What the player sees:**  Transparent version of the amphora; one shard is clearly visible on the table; others are hidden
- **Player action 1:** Drag the shard to its correct position in the transparent amphora
- **What happens 1:** Shard clicks into place; calendar advances to Spring 2019 objects appear on the shelf including ”Barny the Frog” (glows softly)
- **Player action 2:** Tap the frog
- **What happens 2:** Zoom in to the frog → audio story plays (Nina)
- **Player action 3:** Audio ends naturally, or player taps outside the object
- **What happens 3:** Zoom back out to table → a second shard is visible on the table
- other shelf objects can be tapped for their ID card text at any point

<br>
<br>
