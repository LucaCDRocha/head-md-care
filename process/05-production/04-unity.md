# 3D - Unity (01.06.26-15.06.26)

Interactions, sounds, and animations were added in Unity to bring the 3D café (created in Blender) to life. Scripts were used as the main method.

<br>

**Animations & Visual Changes:**
* Doors opening and closing
* Calendar rotating forward and backward in time
* Appearing and disappearing of objects on shelves and tables
* Individual changes for story events, such as: color change for St. Patrick’s Day (everything turns green), appearing and disappearing footprints
* Changing poster motifs on the walls
* Blend-in of “ID cards” (short text descriptions for object stories without audio narration)

<br>

**Camera Movement:**
* Implemented using Cinemachine

<br>

**Two main interaction types:**
* Drag & drop of shards (to repair the amphora)
* Click (open doors, zoom in and out of objects)

<br>

**Sounds:**
* Background sounds: rain outside + indoor café ambience with music, cutlery noises, coffee machine, etc.
* Sound effects: drinking coffee, mug hitting the table, amphora breaking, snapping of shards when placed correctly, time rewind
* Voices: intro and outro with café owner + customers telling object stories
